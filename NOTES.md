# Notes

- **Mobile is deferred.** Layout is tuned for desktop (≥900px). Known phone-width issues, left for later: the HUD's sentence line (earnings, word count, time left) crowds and overlaps; the article does not scroll to the excerpt being typed, so word-score pops for it are skipped while it is off screen.
- **Design direction: stark.** Clutter is lots of small things (chips, tiny caps labels, underlines, offset shadows), not the big ones. Keep the big gestures (the halftone wallpaper, strong type, the article frame) and cut the small ones. New UI should add as little as possible.
  - Links are colour only, never underlined.
  - Buffs show as icon tiles; details live in the hover card.
  - Offset shadows only on the article, dialogs and the main action button.
